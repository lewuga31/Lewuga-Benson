Teaching Philosophy

My teaching philosophy is rooted in a hands-on approach — learning by doing. I emphasize real-world scenarios and actionable solutions that push students to go beyond the classroom, helping them develop practical and tactile experience in filmmaking.

Lighting & Filmmaking Workshop

In this workshop, students learned to use professional lighting equipment such as the Aputure 300X and Nova P300C to create mood and atmosphere in their scenes. We focused on problem-solving through light — translating emotions and story beats visually.

On-Location Film Shoots

Through collaborative short film projects, students explore every stage of production — from pre-visualization to final edit. These shoots encourage adaptability, teamwork, and creativity under real-world conditions, preparing them for professional film sets.
