Synopsis

The Jump follows a young man, Joe Maccalupo, as he confronts a moment of profound uncertainty. Faced with circumstances that feel insurmountable, he must decide whether taking one of the riskiest—and potentially deadliest—jumps of his life could offer a way out, or only deepen the consequences he already faces.

The documentary was shot in Fall 2017 and made possible with the support of Laura Kranning, assistant professor, experimental filmmaker, and mentor at the University at Buffalo, along with Joe Maccalupo, then a senior chemistry major at the university.

The Jump was filmed on an iPhone SE and edited in Adobe Premiere Pro. Audio was captured separately using an Audio-Technica cardioid condenser lavalier microphone. From pre-production through post-production, the entire project was completed over the course of approximately two months.
