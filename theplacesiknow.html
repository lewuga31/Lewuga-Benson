The Places I Know

Producer / Director / DP / All post: Léwuga Benson

2021

Excerpt – The Places I Know Soft ambient sound in the background—train horns in the distance, wind brushing trees. These streets raised me. From Buffalo’s weatherworn corners to the quiet, open skies of Alden and Elma, every mile feels like a memory. I set out with a camera, but found more than just images—I found rhythm in the stillness, beauty in the overlooked. This is more than a city. It’s a pulse, a patchwork of stories stitched into sidewalks and fields. Lancaster’s backroads. Elma’s winding trails. These are the places I know. And this—this is my love letter to home.
