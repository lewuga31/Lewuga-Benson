Synopsis

This Mutt is a short film that samples found footage materials to interrogates the weight of language in America’s racial narrative. Through the lens of this real-life tragedy, the film explores how words have long been weaponized to strip people of their humanity, inflame division, and obscure justice. It's a meditation on the politics of punishment, the echoes of systemic racism, and the legacy of dehumanization that continues to shape lives on both sides of the law.
